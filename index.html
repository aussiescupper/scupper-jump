How to play

Goal. Climb the tower of blocks and land on the golden goal block at the top to clear the level.

Move. A/D or ←/→. Jump. Space, W or ↑ — hold for a higher jump. On a phone, use the pad at the bottom.

Don't fall. The red floor at the bottom is lethal, and it creeps up as you land on higher blocks. Fall into it and you start the climb again — but you can always drop back to a block you have already stood on.

Blocks.

  • Solid — dependable.
  • Sliding — shifts side to side.
  • Crumbling — collapses a moment after you land.
  • Ice — slippery footing.
  • Bouncer — flings you way up.
  • Spikes & sawblades — they cut through whatever they touch.

Fight Pit. Waves of stick figures come at you in a small arena. Punch with F or the fist button; jump first and it lands as a heavier kick. They wind up visibly before they swing, so every hit is dodgeable. Each one you drop pays out, each wave cleared pays a bonus, and it keeps going until you are knocked out.

Smash Lab. A room full of stick figures and no climbing. Drag one about with a finger or the mouse, fling it at the blocks, the spikes or the sawblades, and you get paid for the damage. Bodies restock themselves. Everything you earn goes straight into your Stick Credits.

Endless. One tower, no top, and a red floor that keeps rising. It gets harder the higher you go and stops getting harder at 520m. One life per run — your best climb is kept, and every metre pays Stick Credits.

Go limp. Press R or tap the stickman button at the bottom of the screen and he drops into a ragdoll wherever he is. Press it again to get back up. Flop into the red floor while limp and it counts as a death.

When it goes wrong. The stickman is a ragdoll, and he comes apart where the blade went through — land on spikes and you lose your legs, walk into a sawblade at head height and you lose your head. Whatever is left flops all the way down the tower, painting the blocks on the way. It will not tidy itself up — tap, click or press a key when you have seen enough. Squeamish? Turn Blood & guts off in Settings.

Stick Credits. Collect coins, clear levels fast and death-free, then spend the lot in the Shop on gear, skins and hats. Replaying a cleared level pays 30%.
